# OmniGem Gallery — Preliminary Risk Register (P1 Discovery & Requirements)

Status: DRAFT for Gate G0. Likelihood/Impact are qualitative (Low/Medium/High) pending deeper analysis at Design phase.

| ID | Risk | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Priority | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | PII (name, phone, DOB) collected via lead form/chatbot without a clear consent notice or lawful basis under Decree 13/2023 (PDPD) | Compliance | Medium | High | High | Add explicit privacy notice + consent capture at point of collection (US-C1 AC3); compliance-checker to confirm required notice content and consent mechanism before Design phase | Product Owner + Compliance |
| R-02 | **RESOLVED (classification):** Date of birth is confirmed **basic personal data under Decree 13/2023 Art. 2(3)** — not sensitive, and not upgraded to sensitive by combination with name+phone. Residual risk is narrower: consent must still be captured for DOB (PDPD requires consent for all personal data), and free-text chatbot fields could incidentally capture actual Art. 2(4) sensitive data (see `data-classification.md` item 9) | Compliance | Low | Low | Low | Ensure DOB is covered by the same consent mechanism as other lead fields (Art. 11); apply chatbot minimization/no-solicitation guardrails for sensitive data per `data-classification.md` item 9 | Compliance |
| R-03 | Chatbot/lead-form data stored in a Google Sheet (per research doc §4) has materially weaker access control, audit trail, and encryption than a proper CRM | Security / Compliance | High | Medium | High | Decide CRM vs. Google Sheet before Design phase (PRD Open Question 7); if Sheet is used short-term, enforce restricted sharing, no public link, access logging, and a migration plan to a real CRM | Product Owner + Architect |
| R-04 | **Cross-border data transfer is a firm PDPD Art. 25 obligation, not a contingent question.** Vercel (hosting/logs), Cloudinary (images, low-PII), and Anthropic/Claude API (chat transcripts with PII, possibly sensitive per item 9) all move data outside Vietnam. | Compliance | High | High | High | OmniGem must prepare and maintain a **Cross-Border Transfer Impact Assessment dossier (Hồ sơ đánh giá tác động chuyển DLCN ra nước ngoài) — Art. 25**, filed with the Ministry of Public Security (A05) within 60 days of transfer. See `compliance-scope.md` Obligation B for the per-vendor breakdown and owner. Target: dossier complete before go-live. | Compliance |
| R-05 | Authenticity/certification claims ("100% natural jade," lab-certified) create legal/reputational liability if a product is later disputed as non-genuine | Business / Legal | Low | High | Medium | Only publish certification claims backed by an actual lab certificate per product; keep certificate references traceable in CMS; align with existing Fanpage commitments (`create-facebook-fanpage.md` §2 Q1) | Product Owner |
| R-06 | Zalo platform dependency/lock-in — the entire closing funnel depends on Zalo OA/widget; any Zalo API/policy change (e.g., prefill deep-link syntax, per research doc §5.3) can break the funnel with no owned fallback | Business / Technical | Medium | Medium | Medium | Monitor Zalo Developer docs for breaking changes; keep a phone/hotline fallback CTA alongside Zalo; verify deep-link syntax before each release | Architect |
| R-07 | SEO/organic traffic ramp-up risk — SEO is a stated core growth channel (research doc §6b, phase P4) but organic traffic takes months to materialize; early-stage lead volume may be low without paid channels | Business | High | Medium | Medium | Set realistic KPI expectations for months 1–3 (see PRD §7); consider pairing with Facebook Ads/Fanpage (already documented) during ramp-up | Product Owner |
| R-08 | Image/video weight for high-res jade photography (zoom, light-transmission video per research doc §2) risks poor Core Web Vitals and higher CDN/hosting cost on mobile 4G | Technical | Medium | Medium | Medium | Enforce responsive/lazy-loaded images via Cloudinary/`next/image`; set and monitor a CWV budget (NFR-1); monitor Cloudinary/Vercel cost against ceiling | Architect |
| R-09 | Chatbot gives an incorrect feng-shui/product recommendation or a confidently wrong authenticity/return-policy answer (hallucination) due to weak RAG grounding | Technical / Business | Medium | Medium | Medium | RAG must ground responses in live CMS catalog/FAQ (research doc §4); mandatory human handoff before any purchase commitment (US-D3); periodic review of chat transcripts for accuracy | Architect + Product Owner |
| R-10 | Inconsistent trust/return-policy/certification messaging across Fanpage, chatbot, and website copy erodes credibility | Business | Low | Medium | Low | Single source of truth for policy copy (warranty, certification, return policy); chatbot system prompt and web copy both reference the same content source | Product Owner |
| R-11 | No numeric NFR targets (uptime, accessibility, performance budget, cost ceiling) are defined in the source research — risk of scope disagreement later | Business / Technical | Medium | Low | Low | Resolve PRD Open Question 8 with Product Owner before Design phase sign-off | Product Owner + Architect |
| R-12 | **Missing DPIA dossier obligation.** OmniGem, as data controller, must prepare a DPIA dossier before processing personal data at scale, but no such dossier work is currently planned/scheduled | Compliance | High | High | High | Prepare and maintain a **DPIA dossier (Hồ sơ đánh giá tác động xử lý dữ liệu cá nhân) — Decree 13/2023 Art. 24**, available to / filed with the Ministry of Public Security (A05) within 60 days of starting processing. Target: complete before go-live. See `compliance-scope.md` Obligation A. | Compliance |

## Legend
- **Priority** = combination of Likelihood x Impact, rounded to the higher end when either is High.
- Risks R-01, R-03, R-04, R-12 are the top compliance-relevant items and are carried into `compliance-scope.md` as the primary items for compliance-checker review.
