# Create a Facebook Fanpage

A step-by-step guide to launching a professional Facebook Page for a natural jade & gemstone jewelry brand.

---

## 1. Create the Facebook Page

### Step 1 — Set up a new Page

1. Log in to Facebook using your personal account.
2. Open **Pages** from the left-hand menu (desktop) or the hamburger menu ☰ (mobile).
3. Click **Create new Page**.
4. Fill in the basic information:
   - **Page name** — Use the formula `[Brand name] + [Product type / Unique value]`.
     > *Example: An Ngoc – Natural Jade & Gemstone Jewelry*
   - **Category** — Choose **Jewelry/Watches Store** or **Jewelry/Watches**.
   - **Bio** — Write 1–2 concise sentences that build trust.
     > *Example: 100% certified natural jade & gemstones. Personalized stone-energy consultation based on your birth element.*
5. Click **Create Page**.

### Step 2 — Set up brand identity

- **Profile picture (Avatar)** — *See Section 3: Create logo & images.*
- **Cover photo** — *See Section 3: Create logo & images.*
- **Call-to-Action (CTA) button** — Click **Add a Button** → select **Send Message**.
  > This is the most important button — it lets customers message the shop instantly.

### Step 3 — Complete page details (SEO & trust-ready)

- **Username / @handle** — Go to **Settings → Page setup → Name** and set a no-space, no-accent username matching your brand.
  > *Example: `anngoc.gemstone` → [facebook.com/anngoc.gemstone](https://facebook.com/anngoc.gemstone)*
- **Page details** — Go to **Settings → Page details** and fill in:
  - **Contact info** — Phone / Zalo hotline and business email.
  - **Location (address)** — Enter the store address (if any) to place a map pin on Facebook.
  - **Opening hours** — Choose **Always open** or set your actual working hours.

## 2. Set up the Messenger welcome flow

### Step 1 — Install Meta Business Suite

Download the **Meta Business Suite** app on your phone.

### Step 2 — Open the automation area

1. Open the **Meta Business Suite** app → confirm the correct jewelry Page is selected in the top bar.
2. Tap **Inbox** in the bottom toolbar (the chat-bubble icon).
3. Tap **Automations** in the top-right corner (usually a lightning-bolt or star icon).

### Step 3 — Enable "Instant Reply"

This automatically responds the moment a customer sends their first message.

1. Select **Instant Reply**.
2. Toggle the switch to **On**.
3. Under **Channels**, check **Messenger**.
4. Open **Message** to edit the text content.
5. Paste in the suggested script:

   > Hi [Recipient's name], thank you for your interest in our natural jade & gemstone jewelry!
   >
   > Are you looking for a daily-wear piece, a gift, or advice on choosing a stone based on your birth element? Please share your **date of birth (solar or lunar calendar)** and our specialist will look up the right stone energy and advise you right away!

   > 💡 **Tip:** Tap **Personalize message** below the text box → select **Recipient's name** so the bot greets each customer by name.

6. Tap **Save** in the top-right corner.

### Step 4 — Create Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This creates ready-made buttons in the chat so customers get instant answers.

1. Return to the **Automations** menu → select **FAQ**.
2. Toggle the switch to **On** and select the **Messenger** channel.
3. Tap **Add Question** to create each entry:

   **Q1 — Are your stones/jade certified?**
   > 100% of our products are natural jade and gemstones, each accompanied by a certificate from a reputable gemology lab. Feel free to test the light transmission and natural veining when you receive your order!

   **Q2 — I'd like advice on a bracelet that matches my birth element.**
   > Please send us your date of birth (and time of birth, if known). We'll look up your element / five-element chart and send you the best-matching pieces to consider!

   **Q3 — What is your warranty and return policy?**
   > We let you inspect the item before payment, offer a 7-day exchange for manufacturer defects, and provide lifetime string replacement / stone polishing!

4. Tap **Save** after creating each question.

## 3. Create the logo, avatar & cover photo

### Step 1 — Generate the logo (Brandmark)

1. Go to **[Brandmark](https://brandmark.io)**.
2. Click **Generate** → enter your **store name** → add a few desired **keywords**.
   > *Example keywords: Jade, Gemstone, Amulet, Mineral, Lotus*
3. Choose a **color style** (e.g., *Organic* — as advised by Gemini).
4. Pick a few favorite results → ask **Gemini**: *"Here are a few options Brandmark generated — which one fits best? Should any colors be changed?"* → let Gemini choose the best-fitting logo.
5. Return to **Brandmark** and save the logo Gemini selected.

### Step 2 — Logo & avatar (Canva)

**Create the logo:**
1. Open **Canva** → **Create** → search **"Logo"**.
2. Upload the saved logo → adjust colors as desired.
3. Click **Share → Download** to save it to your device.

**Create the cover photo:**
1. Open **Canva** → **Create** → search **"Cover"** → choose **Facebook Cover (Landscape)**.
2. Ask **Gemini**: *"What should the cover photo include?"* then *"Write a detailed design brief I can paste into Canva to generate the cover. Describe it in detail so Canva produces a realistic, accurate image of what you described."*
3. Paste Gemini's prompt into Canva's **"Describe your ideal design"** field → **Generate** → refine as desired.
